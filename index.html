One CareerPilot. Your Complete College Journey.

The focus changes as you progress — from exploration and foundations to internships and placement readiness.

Year 1 — Explore

Technology awareness, communication, programming basics, aptitude foundations and career discovery.

Year 2 — Build

Core programming, DSA foundations, projects, aptitude and deeper career understanding.

Year 3 — Industry Ready

Advanced skills, projects, internships, resume, LinkedIn, GitHub and mock interviews.

Year 4 — Placement Ready

DSA, aptitude, technical interviews, HR preparation, profile optimization and applications.
