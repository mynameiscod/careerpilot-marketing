What is CareerPilot?

CareerPilot is an AI-powered career-readiness platform that helps students assess, plan, learn, practice and become job-ready through one guided journey.

Assess

Understand your current level across technical and employability skills.

Plan

Get a personalized roadmap built around your year, branch and goals.
